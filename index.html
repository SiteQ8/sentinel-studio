SECURITY KNOWLEDGE

Plain-language guidance for understanding assessment results.

⚑

PRIORITIZATION

Understanding severity

Severity combines potential impact and likelihood. It helps order work, but business context should always inform the final priority.

  • Critical Immediate, material risk requiring urgent action.
  • High Significant impact or likely abuse path.
  • Medium Meaningful weakness with constraints or reduced impact.
  • Low Defense-in-depth opportunity or limited direct impact.
✓

REMEDIATION

Closing findings well

Address root causes, verify changes in a safe environment, and retain concise evidence that demonstrates the control is operating.

  1. Confirm ownership and target date.
  2. Implement the smallest durable fix.
  3. Test expected and negative cases.
  4. Document evidence and residual risk.
◉

RISK GOVERNANCE

Risk acceptance

Acceptance is a time-bound business decision—not a technical fix. Record the accountable owner, rationale, compensating controls, and review date.

Use defined approval thresholds. Reassess when systems, threats, or business impact change.

▤

EVIDENCE

Handling assessment evidence

Collect only what is necessary. Redact secrets and personal data, use access controls, and follow your retention and secure disposal policy.

Evidence should be reproducible, timestamped, scoped, and safe to share with its intended audience.

F-002

Cloud administration lacks phishing-resistant MFA

CriticalIn progressns-productionCloud Platform#identity#cloud#MFA

Overview & impact

If a privileged credential were separately compromised, a weaker second factor may not provide sufficient protection against modern phishing. Administrative access could affect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of fictional cloud resources.

Evidence

Sample configuration review: two fictional emergency administrator accounts permit a legacy one-time-password factor. No authentication attempts were performed.

Recommended remediation

Require phishing-resistant authentication such as passkeys or hardware-backed FIDO2 keys for privileged roles. Restrict emergency accounts, monitor their use, and maintain a tested recovery process.

References

  • CISA: Implementing Phishing-Resistant MFA
